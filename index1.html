Wordle Game
Guess the right word
and jump to the next level

Less attempts = Higher score!

The game has 10 levels. In each level you must guess the correct word
Scoring
Score is based on the n of guesses used to find the word, Fewer guesses = higher score, More guesses = lower score, Final score = total score across all 10 levels.
How to Play
- Type your guess using the keyboard. - Submit with Enter. - Letter colors give you hints: - Green: correct letter, correct position - Yellow: correct letter, wrong position - Gray: letter not in the word - Guess the word correctly to move to the next level. - After level 10, your final score is shown.
